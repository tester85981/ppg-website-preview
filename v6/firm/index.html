The firm

We don’t hand you a plan. We go get the deal.

Preeminent Partners Group is a founder-led strategic partnership firm. You bring the opportunity everyone can see and nobody has time to chase. We own it, from the first call to a working partnership. Every deal gets its own terms. They have to work for you, your partner and us.

  • Preeminent Partners Group LLC
  • Founded and led by Ryan Horning
  • Terms built for each deal

Potential doesn’t show up on a P&L. Deals do.

You’ve seen the money your business isn’t collecting. It sits between sales, operations and finance. Getting it takes research, relationships, a smart deal and follow-through. Nobody owns it, so it stays an idea.

A plan doesn’t change that. A deck doesn’t either. Someone has to find the partner, make the deal and see it through.

That’s the job we built PPG to do.

Preeminent is in our name. It’s also the standard.

It means your long-term interest comes before any single deal. You get no advice until we know your business. You hear what’s worth chasing and what isn’t. And we build deals where the other side wins too. A partner who isn’t winning won’t deliver.

If a deal is wrong for you, you hear it from us before you sign. Even when it costs us the work.

You decide, at every step, if we’ve earned the next one.

What that looks like for you.

  2. 2.

  3. 3.

    Every job has a name on it before work starts. Yours and ours.

  4. 4.

    Nothing goes out in your name without your yes. Not a message, not a list, not a signature.

  5. 5.

    Bad news travels as fast as good news.

  6. 6.

    We grow a deal only when the numbers back it up.

What we stand on.

  • Your next opportunity is usually already inside the business, or one relationship away.
  • Relationships are assets. Treat them that way.
  • You don’t have to own something to profit from it. Access can be enough.
  • A strategy nobody executes is just a document.
  • Incentives drive behavior. Ours included.
  • The best deal is one both sides want to grow.

You deal with the founder.

Ryan Horning founded Preeminent Partners Group. He reads every situation that comes in. He leads every working session. He answers for every engagement, start to finish. No hand-off to a junior team.

We don’t take everything. Here’s how we choose.

What we look for

A specific opportunity. Numbers you can measure. Access to the information and people it needs. Someone on your side who can deliver what the partnership sells. Terms that protect both sides.

What we are not

We are not a law firm or an investment bank. We are not a broker-dealer, a lender, an accounting firm or a restructuring advisor.

We don’t raise capital, sell businesses or broker acquisitions. We don’t do work that needs a license we don’t hold.

When you need those roles, your own advisers stay in their seats. We work alongside them.

Who we’re not built for

Startups. Businesses without customers, without assets, or without someone who can make the call.

If it’s worth chasing, we’ll tell you. If it isn’t, we’ll tell you that too.
