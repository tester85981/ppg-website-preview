Approach

You get the result. You keep the control.

This is how a PPG strategic partnership gets built and run. You see what we handle, what you decide and how the terms get set. Short version: we do the heavy lifting. You make the calls. Nothing starts until it’s in writing.

Every deal is different. So are the terms.

There’s more than one way to build a deal. The right one gives you, your partner and PPG each a real reason to make it work.

If the terms aren’t right for this deal, it doesn’t start.

Six steps. A clear decision at every one.

At each step, we look at the facts with you and decide how to move forward. If demand, the numbers or the ability to deliver change, your written agreement says what happens next.

  1. 1.

    Opportunity Review

    What you get: a straight answer on whether there’s a real opportunity here.

    What we do: read your outline. Hold one working session. Give you a written answer with our recommendation.

    What we need from you: a few sentences, one session, and what only you know.

    The decision: is it real? Do the numbers work? Is there a clear way to get it done?

  2. 2.

    Terms first

    What you get: one written agreement, before any work starts.

    What we put in it:

    • the opportunity and the scope
    • who does what
    • how PPG is paid
    • how results are measured, where that matters
    • approvals, confidentiality and reporting
    • approved costs
    • how long it runs and how it ends

    What we need from you: who decides what, and the information we need to size it up.

    The decision: do both sides approve the agreement? Nothing starts until they do.

  3. 3.

    Check the math

    What you get: the real numbers before anyone gets excited.

    What we do: map what you have, who would want it and what it takes to deliver. Then we test the numbers and name what has to be true.

    What we need from you: access to the information and people the work needs.

    The decision: is there a real business case worth taking to a deal?

  4. 4.

    Find the partner

    What you get: a partner with a real reason to say yes.

    What we do: find the companies that need what you have. Research each one, make the case and qualify the fit. Make contact only the way you approve.

    What we need from you: your OK on every message and every target list. Nothing goes out in your name before that. And your time for the conversations that matter.

    The decision: is there a partner with a real reason to say yes?

  5. 5.

    Build the deal

    What you get: terms both sides want to keep.

    What we do: design what each side puts in and gets back. Pricing and revenue share. Who does what, and when. Exclusivity and risk. Credit for results and reporting. We lead or support the negotiation, as agreed.

    What we need from you: your approval of the terms, and your signature. Only yours.

    The decision: is this a deal both sides would want to grow?

  6. 6.

    Make it run

    What you get: the deal live, and tracked against what we agreed.

    What we do: get it live, clear the friction and track progress.

    What we need from you: deliver what the partnership sells.

    The decision: is it hitting the goal? Keep going, adjust or exit under the agreement.

We carry the work. You keep the decisions.

What your team does

What you can always see

The pipeline. Every partner we talk to. Every message sent in your name. Every decision point. The numbers, the risks and the open issues.

Check our work any time. No black box.

Terms built for your deal.

Every deal is different. Your written agreement sets how PPG is paid and who does what. It sets how results are measured and credited, where that matters.

It also sets approved costs, who decides what, how long it runs and how it ends.

A deal can use a fixed fee or pay tied to performance. It can use a share of revenue or margin, royalties, or a mix. The right one depends on the opportunity, the work, the risk and what each side brings.

The terms have to work for you, your partner and PPG. If they don’t, there’s no deal.

There’s no fee for the Review. Here are the other straight answers.

Are you consultants?

No. We do the analysis. But we’re built to find, build, negotiate and run one specific deal. Not to hand you advice and leave.

Are you a broker?

No. An introduction can be part of the work. The work keeps going after it: the terms, the negotiation, the launch and the tracking.

Do we need to build a team?

No. We carry the work. Your team gives us the information, approvals and delivery only your business can.

How involved will I be?

At every decision that matters, and nowhere else. Before work starts, the agreement spells out three things. What we handle on our own. What needs your approval. How we report.

Will you contact our customers or partners?

Only with your approval, in the way you approve, and within your consent and opt-out rules.

What does the Opportunity Review cost?

There is no fee for the Review.

What if there’s nothing there?

You get it in writing, with the reason. A clear no is worth having.

Can we stop?

Your agreement answers that before work starts. It spells out how long the deal runs and how either side exits.

What about confidential information?

Keep your first outline non-confidential. Before anything sensitive is shared, both sides agree confidentiality terms in writing.
