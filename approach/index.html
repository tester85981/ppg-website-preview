Approach

You get the result. You keep the control.

Here’s how a PPG engagement runs, what we handle, what you decide and how the terms get set. Short version: we do the heavy lifting, you make the calls, and nothing starts until it’s in writing.

Every deal is different. So are the terms.

There’s more than one way to structure a deal. The right one gives you, your partner and PPG each a real reason to make it work.

If the terms aren’t right for this deal, it doesn’t start.

Six steps. A clear decision at every one.

At each step, we look at the facts together and decide how to move forward under the written agreement. If demand, the numbers or the ability to deliver change, the agreement says what happens next.

  1. 1.

    Opportunity Review

    What we do
    Read your outline. Hold one working session. Give you a written answer with our recommendation.
    What we need from you
    A few sentences, one session, and what only you know.

    The question

    Is there a real opportunity here, with numbers that work and a clear way to get it done?

  2. 2.

    Terms first

    What we do
    Put it all in one written agreement: the opportunity, the scope, who does what, how PPG is paid, how results are measured where that matters, approvals, confidentiality, reporting, approved costs, how long it runs and how it ends.
    What we need from you
    Who decides what, and the information we need to size it up.

    The question

    Do both sides approve the agreement? Nothing starts until they do.

  3. 3.

    Check the math

    What we do
    Map what you have, who would want it and what it takes to deliver. Test the numbers and name what has to be true.
    What we need from you
    Access to the information and people the work needs.

    The question

    Is there a real business case worth taking to a deal?

  4. 4.

    Find the partner

    What we do
    Find the companies that need what you have and open the door. Research each one, make the case, make contact the way you approve, and qualify the fit.
    What we need from you
    Your OK on every message and every target list before anything goes out in your name, and your time for the conversations that matter.

    The question

    Is there a partner with a real reason to say yes?

  5. 5.

    Build the deal

    What we do
    Design what each side puts in and gets back: pricing, revenue share, who does what, timing, exclusivity, risk, credit for results, and reporting. Lead or support the negotiation, as agreed.
    What we need from you
    Your approval of the terms, and your signature. Only yours.

    The question

    Is this a deal both sides would want to grow?

  6. 6.

    Make it run

    What we do
    Get it live, clear the friction, and track progress against what we agreed.
    What we need from you
    Deliver what the partnership sells.

    The question

    Is it hitting the goal? Keep going, adjust, or exit under the agreement.

We carry the work. You keep the decisions.

What PPG does

The research, the math, finding the partner, the outreach you approve, the deal design, the negotiation, the coordination and the reporting. All within the agreed scope.

What your team does

Gives us accurate information. Sets the limits for your brand and your operations. Shows up for the decisions that matter. Approves every commitment. Delivers what only your business can.

What you can always see

The pipeline. Every partner we talk to. Every message sent in your name. Every decision point. The numbers, the risks and the open issues. Check our work any time. No black box.

Terms built for the deal.

Every deal is different. The written agreement sets how PPG is paid, who does what, how results are measured and credited where that matters, approved costs, who decides what, how long it runs and how it ends.

A deal can use a fixed fee, pay tied to performance, a share of revenue or margin, royalties, or a mix. The right one depends on the opportunity, the work, the risk and what each side brings.

The terms have to work for you, your partner and PPG. If they don’t, there’s no deal.

Questions worth asking first.

Are you consultants?
No. We do the analysis, but we’re built to find, structure, negotiate and run one specific deal. Not to hand you advice and leave.
Are you a broker?
No. An introduction can be part of the work. The work keeps going after it: the terms, the negotiation, the launch and the tracking.
Do we need to build a team?
No. We carry the work. Your team provides the information, approvals and delivery only your business can.
How involved will I be?
At every decision that matters, and nowhere else. Before work starts, the agreement spells out what we handle on our own, what needs your approval and how we report.
Will you contact our customers or partners?
Only with your approval, in the way you approve, and within your existing consent and opt-out rules.
What does the Opportunity Review cost?
There is no fee for the Review.
What if there’s nothing there?
We tell you, in writing, with the reason. A clear no is worth having.
Can we stop?
How long it runs and how either side exits are written into the agreement for that deal.
What about confidential information?
Keep your first outline non-confidential. Before anything sensitive is shared, both sides agree confidentiality terms in writing for that deal.
