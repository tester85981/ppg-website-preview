What happens next

This is the Opportunity Review, step one of the PPG Deal Method.

  1. You send a few sentences. Nothing confidential.

  2. Ryan reads it. If there’s a fit, you get one working session. If there isn’t, you hear that straight, with the reason.

  3. After the session, you get a written answer. The opportunity. Who would want it. The deal that fits. What has to be true. And whether to go after it, wait or walk away.

No fee for the Review. No obligation to go further. Sending this doesn’t commit you or us to anything.

If you go ahead, there’s still nothing to pay up front. PPG works on performance and is paid only out of what a closed deal creates.
