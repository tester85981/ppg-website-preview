The firm

We don’t hand you a plan. We go get the deal.

Preeminent Partners Group is a founder-led partnership firm. We take the opportunity everyone in your business can see and nobody has time to chase, and we own it, from the first call to a working partnership. Every deal gets its own terms, and they have to work for you, your partner and us.

  • Preeminent Partners Group LLC
  • Founded and led by Ryan Horning
  • Terms built for each deal

Potential doesn’t show up on a P&L. Deals do.

Every established business has money it isn’t collecting. It sits between sales, operations and finance. It takes research, relationships, a smart deal and follow-through. And because no one owns it, it stays an idea.

A plan doesn’t change that. A deck doesn’t either. Someone has to find the partner, make the deal and see it through.

That’s the job we built PPG to do.

Preeminent is in our name. It’s also the standard.

It means we put your long-term interest ahead of any single deal. We understand your business before we tell you anything about it. We tell you what’s worth chasing and what isn’t. And we build deals where the other side wins too, because a partner who isn’t winning won’t deliver.

We earn the right to keep going. Every step.

What that looks like in practice.

  • We pass on weak math.

    If a deal only works on hope, we say so and stop.

  • We know what’s in it for the other side before we call them.

    Nobody hears from us on your behalf without a real reason to say yes.

  • Every job has a name on it, yours and ours, before work starts.

  • Nothing goes out in your name without your yes.

    Not a message, not a list, not a signature.

  • Bad news travels as fast as good news.

  • We grow it only when the numbers back it up.

What we believe.

  • Your next opportunity is usually already inside the business, or one relationship away.
  • Relationships are assets. Treat them that way.
  • You don’t have to own something to profit from it. Access can be enough.
  • A strategy nobody executes is just a document.
  • Incentives drive behavior. Ours included.
  • The best deal is one both sides want to grow.

You deal with the founder.

Ryan Horning founded Preeminent Partners Group. He reads every situation that comes in, leads every working session and answers for every engagement, start to finish. No hand-off to a junior team.

We don’t take everything. Here’s how we choose.

What we look for

  • A specific opportunity.
  • Numbers you can measure.
  • Access to the information and people it needs.
  • Someone on your side who can deliver what the partnership sells.
  • And terms that protect both sides.

What we are not

We are not a law firm, investment bank, broker-dealer, lender, accounting firm or restructuring advisor. We don’t raise capital, sell businesses, broker acquisitions, or do work that needs a license we don’t hold. When you need those roles, your own advisers stay in their seats and we work alongside them.

We’re not built for startups, or for businesses without customers, assets, or someone who can make the call.

If it’s worth chasing, we’ll tell you. If it isn’t, we’ll tell you that too.

Send a few sentences. Ryan reads every one. If there’s a fit, you get one working session, then a written answer with a clear recommendation. If there isn’t, you hear that straight, with the reason. No fee for the Review. No obligation to go further.
