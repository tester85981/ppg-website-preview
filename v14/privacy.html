Privacy

How inquiry information is handled at Preeminent Partners Group.

Preeminent Partners Group (PPG) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, your email address, your organization, your role if you choose to give it, and the contents of your message. We do not collect payment information through this website, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Our hosting provider may record standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and reliability. These logs are not used to build profiles of visitors.

We use Google Analytics to understand how this website is used: which pages are viewed, how long visits last, and visitors’ general location and device. Google processes that information under its own privacy policy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics by installing Google’s browser add-on.

How We Use It

We use inquiry information only to respond to you, to evaluate a potential engagement, and to prepare for a conversation. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who We Share It With

We share information only with the service providers who help us operate this website and our email, and only so they can provide those services. The inquiry form is processed by Web3Forms, and submissions are delivered to our email inbox. We may disclose information if required by law.

How Long We Keep It

If your inquiry leads to an engagement, we keep the information for as long as the engagement and our records require. If it does not lead to an engagement, we delete it after 24 months. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you submitted at any time. Privacy questions and requests go to Ryan Horning at ryan@preeminentpartnersgroup.com.

We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page.
