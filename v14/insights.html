Illustrative Opportunity Patterns

Four situations we see in established businesses, and the questions that decide whether there is a deal. These are illustrative patterns, not descriptions of PPG client results.

Pattern 01 · Dormant Accounts

Revenue Waiting in Your Own Records

Illustrative Pattern · Not a PPG Result

A service provider has former clients, abandoned proposals, and inactive leads. The work is to screen permissioned records, design an approved reactivation, make sure the sales owner follows through, and reconcile the contribution that is actually collected.

The Tests That Decide It

  • Consent
  • Baseline
  • Data quality
  • Unit economics
  • Attribution

Pattern 02 · Underused Production

Idle Capacity Meets a Brand That Needs Supply

Illustrative Pattern · Not a PPG Result

A manufacturer has idle production capacity, and a complementary brand needs reliable supply. The work is to evaluate technical fit, true incremental margin, working capital, quality, and exclusivity, then coordinate an authorized capacity agreement that benefits both.

The Tests That Decide It

  • Technical fit
  • Incremental margin
  • Working capital
  • Quality
  • Contracting authority

Pattern 03 · Complementary Customer Access

Two Businesses, One Buyer

Illustrative Pattern · Not a PPG Result

Two businesses serve the same buyer with different services. The work is to test an endorsement, bundle, distribution, or referral arrangement that protects customer trust, and to verify what is actually collected. Both companies and the customer must benefit.

The Tests That Decide It

  • Customer benefit
  • Trust
  • Referral rights
  • Verified collections

Pattern 04 · Distribution Disruption

When a Distribution Relationship Changes

Illustrative Pattern · Not a PPG Result

A brand faces uncertainty after a change at its distributor. The work starts with contract rights, actual service gaps, feasible alternatives, and the profit picture if nothing changes. The best outcome could be a switch, better terms, a revised arrangement, or no change at all.

The Tests That Decide It

  • Contract rights
  • Service facts
  • Alternatives
  • Counterfactual profit
