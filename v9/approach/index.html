The PPG Deal Method

Six steps. A clear decision at every one.

At each step, you look at the facts with us and decide how to move forward. If demand, the numbers or the ability to deliver change, your written agreement says what happens next.

The method takes one opportunity from an idea to an executed deal, then measures what it earns. You pay PPG nothing to start, and PPG invests its own approved resources along the way.

1. Opportunity Review

What you get:
a straight answer on whether there’s a real opportunity here.
What we do:
read your outline. Hold one working session. Give you a written answer with our recommendation.
What we need from you:
a few sentences, one session, and what only you know.
The decision:
is it real? Do the numbers work? Is there a clear way to get it done?

2. Terms first

What you get:
one written agreement, before any work starts.
What we put in it:
  • the opportunity and the scope
  • who does what
  • how PPG’s share is calculated, from income collected after the deal’s agreed costs
  • how results are measured, where that matters
  • approvals, confidentiality and reporting
  • any capital, delivery work or new overhead on your side, disclosed and agreed up front
  • how long it runs and how it ends
What we need from you:
who decides what, and the information we need to size it up.
The decision:
do both sides approve the agreement? Nothing starts until they do.

3. Check the math

What you get:
the real numbers before anyone gets excited.
What we do:
start with your own numbers, what the asset costs you to carry today. Then build the business case. Map who would want it and what it takes to deliver. Test the numbers and name what has to be true.
What we need from you:
access to the information and people the work needs.
The decision:
is there a real business case worth taking to a deal?

4. Find the partner

What you get:
a partner with a real reason to say yes.
What we do:
research and find the companies that need what you have. Make the case and qualify the fit. Approach them only the way you approve.
What we need from you:
your OK on every message and every target list. Nothing goes out in your name before that. And your time for the conversations that matter.
The decision:
is there a partner with a real reason to say yes?

5. Build the deal

What you get:
terms both sides want to keep.
What we do:
design what each side puts in and gets back. Pricing and how the profit is shared. Who does what, and when. Exclusivity and risk. Credit for results and reporting. We negotiate and help structure it, as agreed.
What we need from you:
your approval of the terms, and your signature. Only yours.
The decision:
is this a deal both sides would want to grow?

6. Make it run

What you get:
the deal live, and its economics measured against what we agreed.
What we do:
coordinate the launch, manage the agreed work, clear the friction and measure what the deal earns.
What we need from you:
deliver what the partnership sells.
The decision:
is it hitting the goal? Keep going, adjust or exit under the agreement.

Strategic Profit Expansion Partnerships

Every PPG engagement runs on performance. No fee to start. No retainer.

What PPG invests. We put our own expertise and resources into developing your opportunity. Finding the deal, negotiating it, any outside help it needs, and making it run. Every amount is approved on our side before we commit it. Where a deal justifies it, PPG can also invest its own capital.

How PPG is paid. For a typical deal, PPG earns a negotiated share of the extra contribution profit the deal brings in. It’s worked out from income actually collected, after the deal’s agreed extra costs. It is not a percentage of your gross revenue.

When the deal saves money. On cost-reduction work, PPG’s share can come from verified net savings instead.

When the deal is different. Licensing, recurring distribution and joint venture deals can carry different participation terms. Your agreement spells out which one applies.

What’s on your side. If a deal needs capital, delivery work or new overhead from you, it’s disclosed up front and agreed separately. You never discover it later.

What PPG doesn’t claim. PPG participates only in the specific deals it originates. That can include defined recurring economics where justified. PPG claims no rights over your unrelated future business.

Your written agreement puts it in plain language before anyone commits. The share. What counts as collected income. Which costs come off first. What each side owes. How either side can end it.

It also sets who does what and who decides what. It sets how results are measured and credited, where that matters. And it sets how long it runs.

The promise. If a deal doesn’t create new value, PPG doesn’t get paid.

Why we can work this way. We’re paid from what a deal creates, not from your budget. So we only invest where the math works for you. If we’re wrong about the opportunity, the cost is ours.

Incentives drive behavior. Ours included. So ours point at one thing: a deal that improves your bottom line.

The terms have to work for you, your partner and PPG. If they don’t, there’s no deal.

There’s no fee for the Review. Here are the other straight answers.

Are you consultants?

No. A consultant hands you a plan and leaves the work to you. If you’ve paid for a plan before, you know where it ends up. We do the analysis too. Then we find the partner, build the deal, negotiate it and make it run. You keep every decision.

Are you a broker?

No. An introduction can be part of the work. The work keeps going after it: the terms, the negotiation, the launch and measuring what the deal earns.

Who is this not for?

Startups. Anyone raising capital, selling a company or brokering an acquisition. Work that needs a license we don’t hold. If that’s you, you hear it straight, before any working session.

Do we need to build a team?

No. We carry the work. Your team gives us the information, approvals and delivery only your business can.

How involved will I be?

At every decision that matters, and nowhere else. You approve. You don’t administer. Before work starts, the agreement spells out three things. What we handle on our own. What needs your approval. How we report.

Will you contact our customers or partners?

Only with your approval, in the way you approve, and within your consent and opt-out rules.

What does the Opportunity Review cost?

There is no fee for the Review.

What does it cost to work with PPG?

Nothing to start, and no retainer. We invest our own approved resources in finding and building the deal. For a typical deal, we’re paid a negotiated share of the extra profit it brings in, from income actually collected after its agreed costs. It’s not a cut of your gross revenue. On cost-saving work, our share can come from verified net savings. If your side needs capital, delivery work or new overhead, you agree to it up front.

Do you take a share of our other business?

No. PPG participates only in the specific deals it originates. Your unrelated future business stays yours.

Why would you do the work before you’re paid?

Because we’re paid from what the deal creates, not from your budget. That keeps us honest about the math. If a deal won’t pay you, it won’t pay us, so you hear that early.

What if there’s nothing there?

You get it in writing, with the reason. A clear no is worth having.

Can we stop?

Your agreement answers that before work starts. It spells out how long the deal runs and how either side exits.

What about confidential information?

Keep your first outline non-confidential. Before anything sensitive is shared, both sides agree confidentiality terms in writing.
